# Product

## Register

product

## Users

OpenDay is used by construction fit-out operators who need verified project truth instead of another task list:

- Admins and management need portfolio visibility across stores, contractors, opening readiness, and risk exposure.
- Project Managers need to know which project needs attention today, why it is at risk, and what action to take.
- Contractors need a focused field workflow for weekly progress reporting, blockers, support needs, and photo evidence.
- Viewers need read-only access to allowed project and dashboard data.

The product is used in operational contexts: office reviews, site coordination, weekly reporting, risk meetings, and pre-opening control. Users are evaluating real construction progress, not managing generic todos.

## Product Purpose

OpenDay is a construction fit-out project control system. It turns contractor reports, PM site visits, Gantt schedules, photos, and portfolio signals into verified operational decisions.

Success means:

- contractor progress claims are captured with owner identity and evidence;
- PM site visits independently verify actual progress;
- contractor vs PM differences are visible and explainable;
- schedule drift and opening readiness are detected early;
- risk intelligence guides attention across the portfolio;
- management can trust dashboard numbers because business logic remains in engines and persistence remains in services.

## Brand Personality

Precise, operational, evidence-driven.

OpenDay should feel like a serious construction project control system: accurate, field-reliable, and grounded in verified project truth. It should not feel playful, decorative, or generic.

## Anti-references

OpenDay must not feel like:

- a generic task tracker;
- a decorative SaaS dashboard;
- a spreadsheet clone;
- a gamified project management tool;
- a colorful startup template;
- a lightweight checklist app.

Avoid interfaces that make progress look subjective, hide uncertainty, or turn construction controls into decorative metrics. OpenDay should feel like an operational control center for construction fit-out projects: progress verification, contractor vs PM comparison, risk visibility, opening readiness, and portfolio control.

## Design Principles

1. Verified truth before activity tracking.
   The interface should emphasize evidence, ownership, comparison, and data confidence over generic task completion.

2. Operational clarity under pressure.
   PMs and management should quickly see what needs attention, why it matters, and what action follows.

3. Comparison is a first-class product behavior.
   Contractor claims, PM observations, photos, risk, and schedule data should be presented as reconciled signals, not separate disconnected records.

4. Field reliability beats decorative polish.
   Contractor and site-visit workflows should be readable, resilient, and fast in real-world conditions, including bright environments and repeated use.

5. Management views must preserve nuance.
   Portfolio dashboards should expose risk, readiness, confidence, and drift without flattening construction reality into generic KPIs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Default target: WCAG AA.

Required product behavior:

- keyboard-accessible workflows;
- reduced-motion support;
- color-blind-safe status signals;
- strong dark and light contrast;
- status and risk information must never rely on color alone.

Use labels, icons, badges, text, hierarchy, and data explanations together so progress, risk, and discrepancy states remain understandable for all users.
